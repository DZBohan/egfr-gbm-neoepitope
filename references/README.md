# References cited in Step 5

Each claim in the report was checked against the paper itself, not against a search result.

## Garfinkle EAR, Perales-Linares R, Gimple RC, et al.

Adjuvant personalized multivalent neoantigen DNA vaccination for MGMT unmethylated
glioblastoma: a phase 1 trial. *Nature Cancer*, 2026.
[10.1038/s43018-026-01163-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-026-01163-w), PMID 42120910,
PMCID PMC13400305. Licence CC BY-NC-ND, file included here.

What the report cites it for: a phase 1 DNA vaccine trial using up to 40 neoantigens per
patient with vaccine-associated T cell responses. The paper vaccinated nine patients with
17 to 40 neoantigens each, reported immune responses in all but one, who was on
immunosuppressive steroids, and 33% overall survival at 24 months.

## Zhang Y, Chi X, Liu K, et al.

Personalized neoantigen-pulsed autologous dendritic cells in newly-diagnosed glioblastoma:
a phase Ib trial. *Nature Communications*, 2026.
[10.1038/s41467-026-75066-w](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75066-w), PMID 42401550,
PMCID PMC13469091. Licence CC BY, file included here.

What the report cites it for: a single-arm phase Ib trial of 11 patients with median
progression-free survival of 16.2 months from surgery. The paper reports median PFS 16.2
months, 95% CI 10.3 to not reached, with PFS defined in its methods as the interval from the
date of surgery, and median overall survival not reached.

## Weller M, Butowski N, Tran DD, et al.

Rindopepimut with temozolomide for patients with newly diagnosed, EGFRvIII-expressing
glioblastoma (ACT IV): a randomised, double-blind, international phase 3 trial.
*The Lancet Oncology* 2017; 18: 1373-85.
[10.1016/S1470-2045(17)30517-X](https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(17)30517-X),
PMID 28844499. Subscription article, no file included here.

What the report cites it for: the phase 3 trial did not improve survival. The paper reports
median overall survival of 20.1 months, 95% CI 18.5 to 22.1, with rindopepimut against 20.0
months, 18.1 to 21.9, in the control group for the minimal residual disease population,
HR 1.01, p=0.93, and the study was terminated for futility at a preplanned interim analysis.

The published article is not open access, so the PDF is not redistributed in this repository.
Green open access copies exist in institutional repositories, for example the University of
Szeged repository, and the article is available through a university subscription.
